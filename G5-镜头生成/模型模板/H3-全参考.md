# H3全参考模板

使用前读取[模型规范H3条目](../模型提示词规范.md#h3)、官方全参考指南和基础指南。只用于Ref2VA。以下为官方六字段骨架，花括号全部替换后交付。

```text
subject_definitions:
{Define each referenced subject and its source, plus separately tracked frame/video/audio roles.}

summary:
[{Official task type or combination}] {Target event, shot flow and main reference relationships.}

retention_analysis:
{One entry for each independently tracked reference, with its official relationship marker and explanation.}

detailed_description:
{One or two English sentences establishing the style.}
[Shot 1] {Framing, angle, placement, subjects, environment, lighting, actions, camera movement, sound and reference activation.}

overall_soundscape:
{Ambient, physical and nonverbal sounds.}

non_diegetic_music:
{Score, or N/A.}
```

可见参考的关系标记为`fully_preserved`、`partially_preserved`、`attribute_transfer`或`weak_reference`；音频为`fully_copy`、`partially_copy`、`reference`或`weak_reference`。任务类型、标签职责、发声源、跨镜对白及编辑视频开头句逐项依官方指南确定。不得将基础三字段或即梦引用写法混入本模式。
